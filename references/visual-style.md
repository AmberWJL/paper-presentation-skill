# House style (from "CS886 Paper 2.pptx", Winter 2026)

Layout: white background, 16:9. Title is the single sentence, top-left, ~32 pt. Slide number bottom-right. Nothing else textual except short labels inside diagrams or a one-line quote in a speech bubble.

Illustration style: flat cartoon with thick dark outlines (characters at desks, two people talking, a person gesturing). Icons are flat with bold black outlines and simple fills (e.g. magnifier with red exclamation).

Palette:
- slate blue 4A6FA5 (primary)
- sage green 8FAF9A (secondary)
- light blue CFE3F0 (fills)
- charcoal 2E3A46 (text / outlines)
- red E63946 (wrong, flaky, problem)
- green 4CAF7A (fixed, accepted)
- grey 9AA5B1 (pruned / inactive)

Recurring motifs:
- A tracker slide repeated every few slides (template: "Threats to Validity" with construct/internal/external and checkmarks accumulating). Mirror it with a pipeline strip for method papers.
- Comparison slides as left/right halves with a character or icon on each side.
- Quotes from the paper in a speech bubble with a small source tag.
- Logos of real tools (e.g. GitHub Copilot) used as-is; otherwise neutral robot variants.

Typography: title ≥32 pt, stat callouts 60–72 pt with 12–14 pt label, diagram labels 12–16 pt. No accent bars, no underlines under titles.
